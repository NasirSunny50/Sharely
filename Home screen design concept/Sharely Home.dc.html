Where the depth went One dimensional moment: the parcel flight on Sending, and the fanned stack with the stamp on Sent. Files are physical cards with a lit top edge, a cast shadow, and a slight rotation as they leave — a perspective transform over flat assets, which is what Flutter can actually draw. Everywhere else stays at one hairline and a 1px shadow. Buttons get a 4px hard bottom edge instead of a blur, so tapping feels like pressing a real key.
